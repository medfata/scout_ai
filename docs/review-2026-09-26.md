> ## Stage 1 — now (each fix with a failing test first, as before)
>
> 1. Stale pacing slots (the one remaining deliverability bug). `messages.scheduled_for` is
>    never cleared, so a message blocked by sending_window, daily_cap or a park keeps a slot
>    that has already passed; at the next window start it skips pacing and sends at once, and
>    every such message wakes inside the same jitter band. Clear scheduled_for whenever a
>    message is blocked by window, cap or park, and also treat an owned slot older than the
>    maximum spacing (9 min) as expired → re-reserve. Workflow test: 10 approved first touches,
>    cap 5, window closing mid-queue → next morning's sends are ≥ 3 min apart and never exceed
>    the cap. Unit test: a stale slot is re-reserved, a fresh future slot is honoured.
> 2. wakeParkedRuns: record why a run is parked (reuse enrollments.next_action_at plus a
>    `parked_reason` column, via drizzle-kit migration) and wake only enrollments parked for a
>    reason the change clears (kill switch turned off → kill_switch; signature/postal address
>    filled → config_incomplete; mailbox reconnected → config_incomplete/mailbox). Settings
>    saves that change nothing relevant wake nothing. Do the waking in a step or `after()`,
>    not synchronously inside the server action, and page past 200.

>    the Gmail adapter (throw ConfigurationError instead) and the stale "structural" comment in
>    sending.ts.

>    unreachable.

> ## gate 4 preview demo in DECISIONS.md and answered Q4)
>
> Scope is exactly section 11 phase 6, assisted mode only (section 0: no automation, no
> unofficial APIs, no Unipile). Design summary to confirm with the owner before building:
> - The manual-linkedin adapter implements Channel: `send()` creates an assisted task
>   (AssistedTask in src/ports/channel.ts) instead of sending. Task creation still goes through
>   sendMessage and its guard (one send path: suppression, kill switch, consent does not apply
>   to LinkedIn but suppression does), with a daily cap of 10 invite tasks
>   (section 0; a `linkedin_invite` send_counters bucket) and the owner's working hours.
> - Inbox gets a LinkedIn tab: open profile, copy message, "Done" / "Skip". Done marks the
>   message sent. Invite tasks carry no note (section 2).
> - Acceptance and replies in assisted mode can only come from the owner: the task card and
>   lead page get "Accepted" and "They replied on LinkedIn" buttons, which resume the lead hook
>   with `accepted` / `reply` events. A reply on any channel stops everything (section 7).
> - Sequence: invite on day 1; message on accept + 1 business day; skip if not accepted within
>   14 days (already modelled in src/domain/sequence.ts). An undone task expires after 3 days
>   like an approval. LINKEDIN_MODE stays `assisted`; eligibility no longer returns
>   `linkedin_not_available` in assisted mode.
> - Q4 (LinkedIn-first variant for leads with no valid email, including catch-all) must be
>   answered before those leads get a LinkedIn-only sequence; until then they are not enrolled.
> - Done when (section 11): assisted tasks appear and complete — proven by workflow tests
